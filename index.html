How a referral works
Four steps, and you can see exactly where your referee is at every stage.
1 Pick an opening Read the description and the eligibility bar before you commit someone's time.
2 Share their details Name, email and phone, plus a short note on why they fit. Resume is optional.
3 They get an invite skyHire emails them a prefilled application link. Nothing enters the pipeline until they apply.
4 You track it live Every stage change shows up in My Referrals, and the bonus is released on joining.

  • Half is released once your referee joins, half after they complete 90 days.

  • Payouts land with your regular salary cycle at the end of the month.
  • A referral stays credited to you for 12 months from the day you submit it.

One document, two places

A folder is a view, not a copy. Every file is stored once against the candidate ID and read by both surfaces, so there is no second store to keep in step.

Upload on the candidate profile, and it is in the folder immediately.
Verify in the Document Centre, and the profile shows Verified.
Two candidates can share a name. The folder is keyed on the candidate ID, never the name.
One candidate applying to several jobs keeps one folder. Each file can name the job it belongs to.
